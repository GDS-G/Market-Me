# AI quote and budget money-unit boundary

Release 1.43 implements the [bounded correction plan](AI_BUDGET_UNIT_BOUNDARY_PLAN.md). [Releases](RELEASES.md) tracks acceptance separately. This is a fail-closed compatibility boundary for the existing hundredths ledger, not arbitrary-currency accounting, exchange conversion, multi-precision ledger support or historical reconciliation.

## Reproduced defect and chosen boundary

Budget editing, reservations, usage and cap comparisons historically use integer hundredths. Rate cards and immutable quote evidence separately carry minorUnitExponent 0–4. reserveQuotedSpend previously copied maximumCostMinor without checking that its scale matched the budget's scale. Four live synthetic pre-fix tests reproduce exponents0/1/3/4 entering the ledger; for a one-major-unit quote and half-unit cap, exponents0/1 incorrectly authorize a hold. The initial test fixture's invalid failover enum was corrected before this reproduction. Tests use no real credentials/provider/charge.

The correction keeps the ledger at its established exponent2. New quoted reservations require exponent2; other quotes remain inspectable at their explicit scale but cannot authorize ledger writes. Current currency equality, quote integrity, expiry, role, capability, source evidence and ordinary cap checks remain independent requirements. There is no rounding conversion between quote scales, currency-code inference of old values, rewritten amount, rehashed quote or changed policy receipt.

Known incompatible historical reservations block new authorization and monetary settlement for their entire workspace/currency. This deliberately includes denied, expired, released and settled rows at any age: filtering only active/current-month rows would hide older ambiguity or let it reenter through an exception. Inspection, acknowledgement, rejection and explicit release remain available. There is no automatic remediation/deletion path; a later separately designed reconciliation must establish historical intent and preserve evidence. A new workspace or a changed currency is not represented as reconciliation of the original records.

## Shared contract and formatting

`packages/domain/src/ai-budget-units.ts` exports:

- `AI_BUDGET_LEDGER_EXPONENT = 2` and `AI_BUDGET_LEDGER_DIVISOR = 100`: immutable primitive constants, not configurable globals. They describe existing application ledger units, not an assertion that every ISO currency uses two decimal places.
- `AI_BUDGET_UNIT_MISMATCH`: safe explanation that a quote remains inspectable but cannot authorize spending with a different scale. `AI_BUDGET_HISTORY_MISMATCH`: safe historical-ambiguity explanation; no private persistence detail is included.
- `isAiBudgetQuoteUnitCompatible(exponent: unknown)`: strict equality to2, with no string/null/number coercion. Invalid, fractional or unsupported exponents fail closed.
- `AiBudgetUnitIntegrity`: ledgerExponent literal2; status compatible/incompatible_history; incompatibleReservationCount integer count. compatible means no known incompatible quote-linked reservation lineage was found, not proof of a correct provider bill, correct historic intent or general currency support.
- `formatAiBudgetMoney(value,currency)`: accepts nonnegative safe integers and a three-uppercase-letter tag, otherwise returns Amount unavailable. It derives whole/fraction using BigInt division/remainder and pads exactly two fraction digits, then replaces the fraction part from Intl.NumberFormat.formatToParts. No floating-point division can lose the final hundredth, including Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER. Locale grouping/symbols remain en-US; no currency conversion or ISO validation occurs. The historical JPY value1 displays0.01 rather than disappearing under locale-default zero-fraction rounding.

Both `AiBudgetStatus` and `AiUsageSummary` now require unitIntegrity. Existing numeric totals and byFeature collections remain raw historical integers for inspection/compatibility; when integrity is not compatible, clients must not interpret them as verified money or compute cap progress. The current web UI suppresses those monetary totals. This is an additive response/type contract but changes what consumers must check; deploy matching UI/API and update external consumers before relying on amounts. Policy-save request/receipt/storage formats, immutable snapshots and version identities remain unchanged.

## Historical lineage inspection

`packages/database/src/ai-budget-unit-integrity.ts` contains the internal read-only `readAiBudgetUnitIntegrity(sql,workspaceId,currency)`. sql is a DatabaseClient or TransactionSql; no actor is accepted because callers already enforce the relevant API/repository authority or are trusted internal reads. The helper is not an unauthenticated HTTP route.

The query counts workspace/currency reservations whose explicit quote exponent differs from2. It left-joins a reservation's spend_exception_request_id to its exception and that exception's denied_reservation_id, then selects the direct cost_quote_id or the denied reservation's quote through COALESCE. This preserves visibility of consumed exceptions whose successor reservation lacks its own quote link. Joins bind workspace as well as identity; another workspace/currency cannot contaminate the result. Unquoted legacy reservations retain the existing hundredths convention; absence of quote lineage is not independent verification of their real-world origin.

The helper returns a fresh object with count and status; no cache, repair, audit, expiration or data mutation occurs. It examines all matching history, not only the twenty recently displayed reservations. getBudgetStatus and getCurrentMonthUsage each attach the result to their current read. This adds two scoped history queries on the AI page; it does not solve the page's wider preexisting query count. Existing workspace/quote-key indexes serve the joins; no schema/index change is introduced.

## Write and provider boundaries

AiRepository's private `assertBudgetUnitIntegrity(transaction,workspaceId,currency)` reads fresh history and throws AiPolicyValidationError with the constant safe explanation when incompatible. Browser metadata is never authorization. The guard is placed before writes in reserveSpend (including incidental expiry), reserveQuotedSpend, settleSpend, requestSpendException, approved decisions and authorizeApprovedSpend. Quote scale is checked before quoted-reservation integrity/replay/expiry work. Existing bad consumed-exception replay cannot reopen spend; use the separate read-only current-state APIs to inspect it.

The release1.42 membership FOR SHARE locks on request/decision/acknowledgement remain intact; this release does not broaden every unrelated membership helper. Existing target locks, writer/approver permissions and workspace isolation are unchanged. The new history query itself is read-only and does not lock an entire ledger. Normal new application writes cannot create incompatible quoted history; arbitrary administrator SQL, trigger disabling or old concurrent writers are outside this guard and must not coexist with the rollout.

prepareWorkspaceTextInvocationIntent and claimWorkspaceTextInvocationAttempt require both quote and rate-card exponent to equal the shared ledger constant inside their existing exact-target, row-locking authorization queries. A fresh history check also precedes new intent/attempt creation. The claim query privately reads currency for this check; destructuring removes it along with prompt hashes from the returned transport target. Existing exact-intent replay may return its original identity without creating authority; provider transport still requires the separate current claim gate.

completeWorkspaceTextInvocationAttempt includes quote/card scale in authorizationCurrent and checks history for a successful outcome. Changed/incompatible evidence converts an otherwise successful response into ambiguous/evidence_changed, discarding output and avoiding automatic usage/settlement. The existing claim-expiry check takes precedence, failure/circuit bookkeeping remains, and this does not pretend an already-sent provider request was canceled or free. Explicit operator resolution with settled_provider_charge also checks history before monetary settlement. confirmed_no_charge release remains governed by the existing evidence/approver workflow; it is not automatic forgiveness of a real bill.

Releasing a hold, rejecting a pending exception, acknowledging an alert and reading prior entities do not reinterpret money and remain possible. An incompatible history flag does not delete rows or grant a bypass. Unit checks are application/repository defenses, not new database-administrator-proof constraints. No new migration, dependency, environment variable, provider credential or execution permission is added; schema remains121, ending at immutable0121.

## UI variables and behavior

AiPolicyPanel derives `unitsVerified` from both usage and budget integrity statuses, exact ledgerExponent2/count0 and matching currencies. Missing or inconsistent metadata fails closed. Its local `money` formatter suppresses monetary display as Unverified amount unless verification matches the current currency; request counts/unit counts remain available. BudgetScope receives this same formatter, preventing a child panel from bypassing the warning. Monthly percentage is calculated only for verified units; an existing cap with unverified units is described as unavailable progress, not incorrectly called absent.

The page explains the established two-decimal ledger separately from quote precision and shows a prominent safe warning for incompatible history or missing verification. Policy inputs retain their original values and the exact-save/revision recovery contract; no stored amount is rewritten. New exception-request/approval handlers and buttons require verified units. Reject and acknowledgement remain independent, and existing retained budget-action current-state lookup is not disabled merely by this new financial guard.

AiQuoteLedger adds `budgetUnitIntegrity` and `budgetCurrency` props. `ledgerCompatible` checks status/exponent/count; `canReserve(quote)` additionally checks current write ability, valid bounded money fields, equal currency and exponent compatibility. Existing status/positive-reservation/link conditions still determine the button. The handler repeats its predicate before network I/O. Other-scale quotes keep their explicitly formatted amounts plus a no-spending explanation; linked reservations say they are not execution authority. Different currency explicitly says no conversion is performed. `validQuoteMoney` bounds exponent0–4, three-letter tag, safe nonnegative ordered integers and maximum1e9; invalid records display Quote amount unavailable instead of crashing or offering reservation.

The AI page also withholds executionAvailable when budget history is incompatible; backend prepare/claim/completion guards remain authoritative. Scoped ai-money-units.module.css notice/warning classes use14px text,1.6 line-height, padding, borders and wrapping; historical ambiguity uses an amber warning without color being the sole signal. A blocked history replaces the generic approval-next-step hint with an explicit verification requirement. This increment does not replace the quote component's older response-loss/idempotency UX with the policy/budget-action recovery system. A quote button is not authority to run a model, and live provider acceptance remains separate.
